About Us

BIGHEAD was founded in 2001

as a creative production house in Hong Kong.

We specialise in video production, design, event media and digital content. With over two decades of production experience, we now combine traditional media production with new AI‑generative workflows to create more flexible, efficient and imaginative content.

From concept development and filming to post‑production, motion graphics, AI‑assisted visualisation and final delivery, we help clients turn ideas into professional and impactful media.

At BIGHEAD, we believe creativity should keep moving forward. By blending human experience with emerging technology, we continue to explore new possibilities in visual storytelling.
